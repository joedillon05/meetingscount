How MeetingsCount decides whether someone was really there
1. A meeting existsLat/lng, start time, length, an allowed radius (500 ft default, wider downtown where GPS bounces off buildings), and a check-in window (±60 min around the start).
2. The participant taps Check inTheir phone reports where it thinks it is plus an accuracy figure. That pin is stored exactly as reported. The server, not the phone, stamps the time.
3. Criteria runEach rule scores the check-in on its own and writes a plain-English reason. Hard rules can reject outright; soft rules only move the confidence score.
4. One of three outcomes, then a human
✓ Confirmed: every rule passed, confidence ≥ 70. Counts.
! Flagged: nothing hard failed but something is off. Goes to the review queue.
✗ Rejected: too far, or outside the window. Also reviewable, and still stored.
Nothing counts or fails against a court order until an officer decides.
